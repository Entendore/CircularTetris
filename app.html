LEADERBOARD

HOW TO PLAY

THE GOAL

Stack pieces around a circle. Fill a complete ring (all segments) to clear it and score points. The game ends when pieces reach the outer edge.

CONTROLS

← → Rotate piece around the circle
↓ Move piece inward (toward center)
↑ Rotate the piece itself
Space Hard drop (instant placement)
C Hold piece for later use
P Pause the game
1-4 Use power-ups

SCORING

Single: 100 × level
Double: 300 × level
Triple: 500 × level
Tetris (4 lines): 800 × level
T-spin Single: 400 × level
T-spin Double: 800 × level
T-spin Triple: 1200 × level
Perfect Clear: 3000 × level bonus
Combo: +50 × combo × level for consecutive clears
Back-to-back: +50% bonus for consecutive Tetrises or T-spins

T-SPINS

Rotate a T-piece into a tight space after a hard drop. T-spins award massive points and count as back-to-back eligible moves!

POWER-UPS

💣 Bomb: Destroy blocks in the outer rings
⚡ Clear: Instantly clear the lowest filled ring
🐌 Slow: Halve gravity speed for 10 seconds
👻 Ghost: Next piece passes through existing blocks

Earn power-ups every 10 lines cleared.

GAME MODES

Marathon: Endless play, level up every 5 lines
Sprint: Clear 40 lines as fast as possible
Ultra: Score max points in 2 minutes
Zen: No gravity, play at your own pace

SETTINGS

Lock delay gives you time to slide a landed piece. It resets up to 15 times per piece to prevent infinite stalling. Set it to 0 for instant locking.
